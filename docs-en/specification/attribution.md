## Provenance

- The baseline JSON Schemas derive from UCP `2026-04-08`.
- The namespace `dev.ucp.*` was renamed to `br.dev.bcp.*`.
- The schema authority `ucp.dev` was renamed to `bcp.dev.br`.
- Brazilian extensions and the Pix handler are original BCP additions.
